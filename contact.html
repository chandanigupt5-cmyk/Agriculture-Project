FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions about AgriVision.

🌱

What is AgriVision?

AgriVision is an educational web project that presents agriculture information, crop details, sustainable farming ideas, and a farm dashboard.

📊

What is the dashboard used for?

The dashboard provides example farm statistics, crop status, environmental information, irrigation details, and a yield estimation tool.

🧮

Are the yield calculations real?

The calculator provides educational estimates. Actual crop yield depends on many factors including soil, weather, crop variety, management, and location.
