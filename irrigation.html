Smart Irrigation

Learn how modern irrigation systems can help farmers provide water efficiently, protect crops, and manage valuable water resources.

SMART WATER MANAGEMENT

What is Irrigation?

Irrigation is the artificial application of water to agricultural land to support crop growth when rainfall is insufficient or irregular.

Modern irrigation systems can use pumps, pipes, sprinklers, drip lines, sensors, and automated controls to deliver water according to crop and field requirements.

  • Provides water when rainfall is insufficient
  • Supports healthy crop growth
  • Can improve water-use efficiency
  • Helps maintain consistent soil moisture
  • Supports agricultural productivity
IRRIGATION METHODS

Types of Irrigation

Different irrigation methods are suitable for different crops, soils, climates, and farm conditions.

01
💧

Drip Irrigation

Drip irrigation delivers water slowly and directly near the root zone of plants through pipes and emitters.

02
🌧️

Sprinkler Irrigation

Sprinkler systems distribute water over crops in the form of small droplets, similar to rainfall.

03
🌊

Surface Irrigation

Water flows across the soil surface and infiltrates into the soil. It is one of the traditional methods of agricultural irrigation.

04
🚜

Center Pivot

Center-pivot systems use rotating equipment to distribute water over large agricultural fields.

SMART AGRICULTURE

How Smart Irrigation Works

Smart irrigation combines sensors, data, and automated controls to improve irrigation management.

🌱

1. Monitor Soil

Soil-moisture sensors can measure the amount of water available in the soil.

📡

2. Collect Data

Sensors can send field information to a digital system for monitoring and analysis.

🤖

3. Automate Irrigation

Automated controllers can operate irrigation equipment based on predefined conditions.

SMART IRRIGATION SYSTEM

Main Components

A smart irrigation setup can contain several connected components working together.

01

Soil Moisture Sensor

Measures soil moisture to help determine irrigation requirements.

02

Water Pump

Moves water from a source through the irrigation network.

03

Controller

Controls irrigation equipment according to programmed settings or sensor information.

04

Drip Lines

Deliver water close to plant roots for targeted irrigation.

Proper irrigation management can help farmers use water more efficiently while maintaining suitable moisture conditions for crops.

💧

🌱

FARMING TIPS

Smart Irrigation Tips

Simple practices for better irrigation management.

💧 Check Soil Moisture

Check soil moisture before irrigation instead of watering on a fixed schedule alone.

🌅 Choose the Right Time

Irrigation timing should consider weather, crop requirements, soil conditions, and evaporation.

🔧 Maintain Equipment

Regularly inspect pipes, pumps, emitters, sprinklers, and connections for leaks or damage.

🌱 Know Your Crop

Different crops have different water requirements during their growth stages.

🌦️ Monitor Weather

Consider recent and expected rainfall before applying additional irrigation.

📱 Use Technology

Sensors and digital tools can help monitor field conditions and improve irrigation planning.
