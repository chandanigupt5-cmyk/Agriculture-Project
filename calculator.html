HOW IT WORKS

Simple Agriculture Formulas

Understand how the estimates are calculated.

🌾

Crop Yield

Estimated production = Farm area × Estimated yield per hectare.

🌱

Seed Requirement

Estimated seed = Farm area × Seed rate per hectare.

🧪

Fertilizer

Estimated fertilizer = Farm area × Fertilizer rate per hectare.

💰

Revenue

Gross revenue = Production × Price per tonne.
