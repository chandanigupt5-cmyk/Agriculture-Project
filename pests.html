🐛 Smart Crop Protection

Pest & Disease Management

Learn how to identify common agricultural pests and crop diseases, understand their causes, and use safe and effective management practices to protect your crops.

CROP PROTECTION

Protect Your Crops

Early identification and proper crop management can help reduce crop losses and maintain healthy agricultural production.

🔍

Early Detection

Regularly inspect leaves, stems, roots, fruits, and soil for unusual spots, insects, discoloration, wilting, or other signs of crop problems.

🌱

Healthy Crops

Healthy soil, balanced nutrition, appropriate irrigation, sanitation, and suitable crop varieties can reduce crop stress and improve resilience.

🛡️

Integrated Management

Integrated Pest Management combines monitoring, prevention, biological methods, cultural practices, and carefully selected controls when necessary.

COMMON PESTS

Major Agricultural Pests

Some common pests that can affect agricultural crops.

🐜 Aphids

Small insects that feed on plant sap and may cause curled, yellowing, or distorted leaves.

🐛 Caterpillars

Larvae of moths and butterflies that can chew leaves and damage young shoots and fruits.

🪰 Whiteflies

Small sap-feeding insects that can weaken plants and may contribute to the spread of some plant diseases.

🪲 Stem Borers

Insect larvae that tunnel into stems of crops and can interfere with plant growth and nutrient movement.

CROP DISEASES

Common Crop Diseases

Crop diseases may be caused by fungi, bacteria, viruses, nematodes, or unfavorable environmental conditions.

01

🍂 Fungal Diseases

Fungal pathogens can cause leaf spots, blights, rots, wilting, and other symptoms. Moist conditions may favor some fungal diseases.

02

🦠 Viral Diseases

Plant viruses can cause mosaic patterns, yellowing, stunting, leaf distortion, and reduced plant growth.

03

🧫 Bacterial Diseases

Bacterial infections may produce water-soaked lesions, spots, rots, wilting, or tissue damage depending on the crop.

04

🌱 Root Diseases

Root problems can reduce water and nutrient uptake and may result from pathogens, poor drainage, or other soil-related conditions.

SMART PROTECTION

Integrated Pest Management

Integrated Pest Management, or IPM, focuses on preventing and managing pests while minimizing unnecessary risks to people, beneficial organisms, and the environment.

🔎

Monitor Regularly

Inspect crops frequently and record pest levels and symptoms.

🌾

Cultural Practices

Use crop rotation, sanitation, resistant varieties, and suitable planting practices.

🐞

Beneficial Organisms

Encourage natural enemies and beneficial insects where appropriate.

🛡️

Appropriate Control

When intervention is needed, choose suitable and legally approved methods according to the crop and pest.

FARMER GUIDE

Pest Prevention Tips

Simple practices that can help maintain healthier crops.

🌱 Use Healthy Seeds

Start with quality planting material from reliable sources and select varieties suited to local growing conditions.

🔍 Inspect Plants

Check the underside of leaves and young shoots regularly for insects, eggs, discoloration, and other symptoms.

💧 Manage Irrigation

Avoid unnecessary irrigation and maintain suitable drainage to reduce conditions that can favor certain diseases.

♻️ Remove Infected Material

Where appropriate, remove and safely dispose of heavily infected plant material to reduce further spread.

🐞 Protect Beneficial Insects

Encourage pollinators and natural enemies by maintaining suitable habitats and avoiding unnecessary broad-spectrum treatments.

📱 Keep Farm Records

Record pest observations, crop stages, weather conditions, management actions, and results to improve future decisions.

IMPORTANT

Identify Before You Treat

Similar symptoms can have different causes. Confirm the pest or disease and follow local agricultural guidance before using any pesticide or other treatment.
