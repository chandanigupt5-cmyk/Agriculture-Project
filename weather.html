FARM ADVICE

Weather-Based Farming Tips

Use weather information as one factor when planning agricultural activities.

💧 Irrigation

Check rainfall forecasts and soil moisture before irrigation to reduce unnecessary water use.

🌱 Planting

Plan planting according to crop requirements, soil conditions and suitable weather.

🌧️ Heavy Rain

During heavy rainfall, ensure proper drainage to reduce waterlogging around crop roots.

🌬️ Strong Wind

Avoid spraying during unsuitable windy conditions and protect vulnerable crops.

☀️ Hot Weather

Monitor crops and soil moisture during prolonged hot and dry periods.

🐛 Disease Monitoring

Regularly inspect crops when warm and humid weather creates favorable conditions for certain diseases.
