FARMING BASICS

What Crops Need to Grow

Healthy crop growth depends on several environmental and management factors.

☀️

Sunlight

Plants need light for photosynthesis and healthy growth. Different crops have different light requirements.

💧

Water

Adequate water is essential, but irrigation should be managed carefully to avoid waste and waterlogging.

🌱

Healthy Soil

Soil provides plants with nutrients, water, physical support, and a suitable environment for roots.

🌡️

Temperature

Crops have specific temperature ranges in which germination and growth are generally most successful.

🧪

Nutrients

Plants require essential nutrients for growth, development, flowering, and crop production.

🐝

Pollination

Many crops depend on pollination by insects, wind, or other natural processes to produce seeds and fruit.

FARMING TIPS

Better Crop Management

🧑‍🌾

Select Suitable Crops

Consider local climate, soil, water availability, market demand, and farming resources when selecting crops.

🔬

Test Soil

Soil testing can help identify nutrient levels and guide appropriate soil-management decisions.

🌧️

Monitor Weather

Weather information can help farmers plan irrigation, planting, harvesting, and crop protection activities.

🐛

Monitor Pests

Regular crop monitoring helps identify pests and diseases early and supports integrated pest management.

♻️

Use Sustainable Methods

Crop rotation, composting, efficient irrigation, and conservation practices can support long-term farm health.

📱

Use Technology

Digital tools can help with farm records, weather monitoring, crop tracking, and decision-making.
